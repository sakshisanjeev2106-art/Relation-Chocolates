Our Story

Our story began in a small kitchen, making chocolates with toy moulds and a lot of love. What started as a simple homemade experiment has grown into a place for handcrafted chocolates, cakes, breads, pizza bases, and all kinds of fresh baked goods made to share, gift, and enjoy.

Relation Chocolates was born from a love of slow, thoughtful craftsmanship. Every recipe in our kitchen is made with care, from handcrafted chocolates and rich cakes to fresh breads, pizza bases, and other baked treats. Whether it's something sweet, comforting, or made for a special occasion, we aim to create food that feels homemade, memorable, and full of heart.
